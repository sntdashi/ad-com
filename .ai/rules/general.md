# General

## Students are customers, technicians are a dedicated team

AD Com has four account roles: admin, supervisor, technician, and customer. Technicians are explicitly appointed members of the dedicated AD Com service team; students have no special role and use the product as ordinary customers. Never describe students as technicians or map service delivery to student grades or competencies.

## Six-role operational model

The current AD Com role model has six accounts: operator (super admin), admin (operations), supervisor, technician, warehouse, and customer. This supersedes the earlier four-role count. Students remain ordinary customers unless explicitly appointed as technicians; warehouse users manage inventory, and only operators may grant operator-level access.

## Use a restrained AD Com identity across web and native UI

Use the supplied AD Com logo and a red accent with neutral surfaces across web and mobile. Keep glass effects subtle on controls, not every content card. Prefer clear Indonesian task labels, readable text, consistent spacing and real service data; avoid decorative sparkle badges, exaggerated marketing jargon, fabricated activity metrics and generated scenes presented as actual school/team photos. Present AD Com as the TEFA TKJ service portal; students remain customers unless appointed to the technician team.
